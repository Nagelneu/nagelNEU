Sound familiar?

It usually happens before you even think about it.

Nail biting rarely has just one cause. Many people recognise themselves in one or more of these situations. Yours may be quite different.

⚡

When the pressure rises

A deadline, a difficult conversation, too much at once. Your hand drifts to your mouth as if it could briefly release the tension.

🕰️

When nothing is happening

Waiting, scrolling, watching TV. Your hands have nothing to do and find something on their own.

🧠

When you are concentrating

While reading, studying or thinking, your mind is busy and your hand carries on by itself.

👀

When you only notice afterwards

Often you only realise once the nail is shorter. That is not a sign of weakness, it is typical of an ingrained habit.

🤲

When you want to stop but do not know how

Good intentions alone often fall short, because the movement is faster than the thought. What is missing is a concrete alternative for exactly that moment.

That is why NagelNEU does not rely on willpower.

It relies on a short routine you can practise until it kicks in by itself in difficult moments.

The method

HANDS STOP: four steps for the automatic moment

HANDS STOP (in German: HÄNDE-STOPP) is a practice routine that interrupts the reach to your mouth. You can use it anywhere, without any tools, in about a minute.

  1. 01

    Notice

    What is happening right now?

    You notice the movement and name it to yourself: “My hand wants to go to my mouth.” That alone is a success.

  2. 02

    Pause

    Can I interrupt it briefly?

    Clench your fists for 3 seconds, release, lay your hands flat. Breathe in for 4, out for 6. The wave usually gets smaller quickly.

  3. 03

    Choose an alternative

    What can my hands do instead?

    Hold, squeeze or care for something: a stress ball, a pen, hand cream, a nail file. Decided in advance, it is easier when it counts.

  4. 04

    Reflect

    What did I notice?

    A quick look back: where was I, what set it off, what helped? Over time you will recognise your pattern.

Good to know: HANDS STOP is a practical exercise routine. It draws on elements that are also used in behavioural therapy for habits like nail biting, but it is not a medically tested procedure in itself.

The 21-day programme

21 days of practising, noticing and noting

The programme takes you through the HANDS STOP method over three weeks. Each day there is a short session that adapts to your triggers and entries.

Who is it for?

Adults who have bitten their nails or picked at their cuticles for a long time and want to change that without pressure.

What do you do?

Every day you see through a typical excuse, answer two short questions, do a daily task and write or speak a few lines in your diary. For acute moments there is the “Caught it!” button and the 60-second HANDS STOP.

How much time?

About five minutes a day, plus the moments when you practise. Each new day opens 24 hours after the previous one.

What is included?

21 daily missions, days for your personal triggers, a diary with voice input, photos on days 1, 7, 14 and 21, an emergency card, a printable hands plan and, at the end, your diary as a PDF. In English and German.

How do you see progress?

You count how often you catch yourself and how often you bite anyway. Your daily goal drops step by step. Charts, photos and your diary show you what is changing.

What do you need?

A smartphone or computer with an up-to-date browser. A nail file, hand cream and something for your hands, such as a stress ball, are helpful.

What can you expect? realistic

More awareness of your moments, concrete alternatives and a plan for difficult days. How quickly things change varies a lot. Setbacks are part of it.

What are the limits? important

NagelNEU is a self-help companion, not a treatment, and there is no guarantee of success. If there is inflammation, significant distress, or if the biting is linked to anxiety or compulsions, please seek medical or psychotherapeutic support.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I stop biting my nails?

Many people manage to bite much less often or stop altogether. How quickly and how completely that happens varies a lot. It helps not to rely on willpower alone: know your triggers, notice the moment earlier and have a set alternative for your hands. That is exactly what you practise with HANDS STOP.

What if I bite my nails again one day?

Then you carry on. A slip is information, not a verdict. File the nail smooth so no edge tempts you, briefly note what was going on, and start again at the next moment. The programme has its own routine for this, and your daily goal adapts.

How does the 21-day programme work?

You set up your profile in a few minutes: your triggers, your reasons, your start date. Then a session of about five minutes opens each day: see through a typical excuse, answer two short questions, do a daily task and write or speak a few lines in your diary. What you enter shapes the following days. At the end you can save your diary as a PDF.

What do I do when I am stressed?

Under stress, a counter-movement that does not fit with biting helps: clench your fists firmly, release, lay your hands flat and breathe out for longer than you breathe in. Keep something to squeeze within reach, such as a stress ball.

Do I need any special materials?

No. A nail file, hand cream and something for your hands, such as a stress ball or spinner ring, are helpful. Some people also use a plaster or bitter nail polish as a noticeable reminder. For the programme, you only need a smartphone or computer with an up-to-date browser.

Is the programme suitable for adults?

Yes, NagelNEU is written for adults, especially people who have been biting their nails for a long time. It is not designed for children.

What can I do if the habit is very strong?

If your nail beds often bleed or become inflamed, if it causes you a lot of distress, or if nothing improves despite many attempts, talk to your GP. Behavioural therapy can help. You can use NagelNEU alongside it.

Does the programme replace professional treatment?

No. NagelNEU is a self-help companion, not a medical device. It does not diagnose anything and does not replace medical or psychotherapeutic treatment.

What does NagelNEU cost?

The programme is free, with no subscription and no contract. When you start, I ask for your email address and for you to follow my YouTube channel. If you like, you can support the project voluntarily.

What happens to my data?

Your entries, photos and voice notes stay in your browser on your device. They are not transferred to NagelNEU, and you can delete them in the app at any time. See the privacy policy (German) for details.
