HANDS STOP worksheet

One sheet for seven days: note when you bite your nails, what you noticed and what helped. After a week you will see your pattern.

Back to the method

How to use the sheet

  1. Write down every moment your hand wanted to go to your mouth, even if you only noticed afterwards.
  2. Tick whether you were able to pause. It is not about perfection, it is about noticing.
  3. At the end of the week, look at your entries: which two situations come up most often?

HANDS STOP

  1. Notice “My hand wants to go to my mouth.”
  2. Pause Fists 3 sec., release, hands flat. 4 in, 6 out.
  3. Choose an alternative Stress ball, pen, cream, nail file.
  4. Reflect What set it off?
